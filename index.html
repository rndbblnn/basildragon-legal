Basil Dragon — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 16 July 2026 · App: Basil Dragon (com.basildragon.app) · Publisher: Basil Dragon · Contact: waterseller@gmail.com

Basil Dragon helps you track your groceries and reduce waste. To do that it reads photos of your receipts and your fridge/pantry using AI, and keeps an approximate list of what you have. We designed it to hold as little as possible:

  • Your photos are processed, not stored. Receipt and pantry photos are sent to our AI providers to read them; we do not store the images.
  • We do not sell your data and there is no advertising.
  • Your data lives on servers in the United States.
  • You can export or delete everything at any time, from inside the app.

We do not collect: your name, precise location, contacts, advertising IDs, or device fingerprints. The app contains no analytics or tracking SDK and no in-app purchases at this time.

AI processing (third parties)

To read photos and text, we send them to:

  • Google (Gemini) — reads receipts and photos, extracts text/items.
  • Anthropic (Claude) — used for harder cases.

You are told this and asked to agree before your first upload (in the in-app consent screen). If you decline, the app runs in manual mode and no photos or text are sent for AI processing. Neither provider uses your content to train their models under our API terms, and the images are not retained by us.

Where your data is stored

Your account and grocery data are stored on servers in the United States (hosting: Railway; database: Supabase). If you are in Québec/Canada, this is a transfer outside your province/country. We are completing a privacy impact assessment of this transfer for adequate protection, as required under Québec Law 25.

Your rights (Québec Law 25 / applicable law)

  • Access / portability — export all your data from the app (Settings → Export).
  • Erasure — delete your account and all associated data from the app (Settings → Delete). Deletion cascades server-side and is not recoverable.
  • Both are protected by your household access token (a request without it is refused).

De-identified, product-keyed reference data (e.g. that "IGA milk 2%" maps to a canonical product) may be retained after deletion because it contains no information about you.

Children

Basil Dragon is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from children under the age required by local law.

Changes

We will update this policy as the app evolves (for example, if we later add analytics or subscriptions) and update the effective date. Material changes affecting already-collected data will be surfaced in-app.
